# v1.2.2

Date: 2026-08-25

- Fixed: a `FloatingWindow` that mounted once at a zero viewport (hidden tab, prerender, offscreen iframe) came back 2px wide forever. The fit-to-viewport clamp measured against `innerWidth` 0 and committed 0x0, the render-phase mirror wrote it into `floatingWindowMap`, and on the next load the stored geometry outranked the `size` prop.
- Changed: `viewportUnusable()` short-circuits the clamp and the maximize/snap effect; a size below `MIN_WINDOW_SIZE` (24px) is never mirrored into the map (position is unaffected); already-damaged records are repaired in place from the `size` prop at mount and on late hydration, then touched once outside the render phase.
- Verification: 56 Jest suites / 256 tests (4 new, 3 failing before the fix), both typecheck configs, probes, and the live stand with hand-corrupted 0x0 localStorage entries.
